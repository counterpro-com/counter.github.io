Tasbih counter questions

Can I use a digital counter for daily zikr?

Yes. A digital counter provides a straightforward way to keep track of repetitions during personal zikr or dhikr sessions.

Can I use it for different recitations?

You can start a separate counting session for each recitation and reset the count when you move to another one.

Does counting replace the practice itself?

No. It is only a counting aid; use it in whatever way best supports your own practice.
