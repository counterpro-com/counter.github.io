Why Use a Digital Tasbih Counter?

A digital counter is easy to carry, quick to reset and helps you focus on zikr. Counter Pro supports multiple counters for different duas and daily goals.

Choose a simple goal for the session

You may decide on a number of repetitions, a set number of rounds, or no numeric goal at all. If using a target, make sure one tap matches the unit you have chosen. For example, count each repetition individually or tap once after a complete round, but avoid switching between those methods mid-session. A clear rule makes the final total easier to understand.

Keep your attention on the practice

Use the screen only as much as needed to keep your place. A quiet setting, readable display, and phone placed within easy reach can reduce interruptions. If a notification arrives or you become unsure of the count, pause and resume calmly from a number you trust. There is no need to rush to recover a missed tap.

Close the session thoughtfully

When you finish, note the total only if you want to refer to it later. A simple counter may not provide a dated history or backup, so write down important information separately and verify what the current app version supports. Reset when you are ready to begin a distinct session. The counter is a tool for counting; adapt it to your own practice and preferences.
