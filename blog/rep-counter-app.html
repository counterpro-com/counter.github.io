Set up each exercise separately

Before a set begins, choose the exercise and confirm the counter is at the expected starting value. Count completed repetitions rather than partial movements, and keep your pace controlled. If your workout plan calls for a rest, take it; a rising number is not a reason to skip recovery.

Separate exercises and sets

Before each set, confirm that the counter represents the right movement and that its starting value is correct. Count a completed repetition once, then pause for the rest period in your training plan. When you switch exercises, record the old total and start a new count so the numbers do not get mixed together.

Keep a useful workout record

A rep counter alone does not tell you which exercise you performed, how much weight you used, or how the set felt. If you review progress over time, write those details in a workout log alongside the total. Keep your phone away from moving equipment and do not reach for it during a lift. Stop if you feel pain or unwell, and follow suitable training guidance.
