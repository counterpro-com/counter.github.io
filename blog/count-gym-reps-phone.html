How to Count Gym Reps Easily Using Your Phone

A basic counter can help you track repetitions while keeping your workout routine uncomplicated.

Prepare before your set

Open a counter before you start moving and place your phone somewhere stable and easy to reach. Choose one exercise and one set for the count. If touching the phone would interrupt your movement or create a safety issue, keep it away and count another way.

Plan the set before you begin

Decide the exercise and intended set before starting the count. If you are alternating movements, finish and record the first movement before opening a separate count for the next. This keeps totals interpretable later and avoids trying to remember which exercise a number belonged to.

Count each completed repetition

Tap once after a completed rep, maintaining good form and a controlled pace. Do not let the number on the screen push you to rush or continue through pain. Stop if you feel unwell and follow the guidance of a qualified trainer or clinician where appropriate.

Make the phone safe to use

Keep the device on a stable surface where it cannot fall into your movement area. Do not reach for it during a lift, while using equipment, or when doing so would compromise balance or form. You can count on paper or ask a training partner if tapping a screen is inconvenient. Treat the tally as a record, not a reason to exceed your planned repetitions.

Separate exercises and sets

Record or remember the completed set total, then reset before the next set. Use a fresh count when you switch to a different exercise. Counter Pro can be used for straightforward rep tallies; see the rep counter guide.
