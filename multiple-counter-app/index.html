Multiple counter questions

Why use separate counters?

Separate counters make it easier to distinguish totals for different activities, such as workouts, routines, or stock checks.

Can I use one counter for unrelated tasks?

You can, but starting a separate count for each task makes the totals easier to interpret.

Should I save important totals elsewhere?

Yes. Record any totals you need to keep in your notes or the relevant tracking system before resetting.
