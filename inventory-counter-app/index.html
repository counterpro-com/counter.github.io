Inventory counter questions

Is Counter Pro a full inventory management system?

No. It is a counter for tallying quantities and does not replace product catalogs, stock alerts, or inventory records.

Can I count separate products?

Run and record a separate count for each product or group of items you need to check.

What should I do with the final total?

Enter it into the stock list or business system you use to maintain inventory records.
