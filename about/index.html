About Counter Pro

A simple counter for everyday tasks.

Counter Pro is an Android counter app for quick, clear tallies across routines, workouts, spiritual practice, study, and everyday work. It keeps the focus on the number you need to track.

View on Google Play

Made for straightforward counting

Some tasks need a quick total, not a complicated setup. Counter Pro focuses on a simple counting flow: choose what you are counting, update the total, and start a fresh count when you are ready.

Daily routines

Keep track of repeatable actions such as reading sessions, study blocks, or completed tasks.

Workouts

Use a manual tally for repetitions, rounds, or sets while following your own training plan.

Everyday tallies

Count items, scores, visitors, or other events when a simple total is what you need.

A clear counting routine

A consistent method helps make a tally easier to trust and understand later.

Decide what counts

Choose one activity and define what a single increment represents before you begin.

Update as you go

Increase the count when the event happens instead of relying on memory at the end.

Check before resetting

Confirm the final total and record it elsewhere if you need a lasting record.

One simple tool, many everyday tallies

A counter is most useful when the task can be represented by a clear, repeatable number.

Personal routines

Count reading sessions, completed tasks, or study blocks. Add a note or use a planner when you also need dates and context.

Spiritual practice

Use a manual tally to keep your place during zikr or tasbih, at a pace that suits your own practice.

Shops and events

Support a quick manual tally of stock or visitors, then transfer confirmed totals to the appropriate record.

Different counter looks

The uploaded design collection includes several visual finishes for the counter face.

Built for simple counts, not complex records

A tally gives you a number. It does not automatically explain what happened, when it happened, or why the total changed.

For important work totals, inventory reconciliation, or records that need review by someone else, use the recordkeeping method your organization requires. Before relying on history, backup, or export, check the options in the app version installed on your device. These capabilities can change between releases.
