Habit counter questions

What habits work well with a counter?

Count repeatable actions such as reading sessions, completed study blocks, walks, or water servings.

Can a counter track a daily streak?

A tally records counts; it is not a calendar or automatic streak tracker. Use it alongside your preferred daily planning method.

How should I keep a habit goal manageable?

Start with one clear action and a small target you can review and adjust over time.
