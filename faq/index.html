FAQ

Counter app questions answered.

Answers about getting started, choosing what to count, and checking which options are available in your installed app version.

What is Counter Pro?

Counter Pro is an Android counter app for keeping simple manual tallies, such as routines, repetitions, scores, items, and other events.

Is Counter Pro free to download?

Check the current Google Play listing for the app's price, in-app purchases, ads, and availability, as these details can change.

Which devices can use Counter Pro?

Counter Pro is listed as an Android app. Check Google Play on your device for current compatibility and minimum requirements.

Can I keep separate counts for different tasks?

Counter Pro is designed for different counting activities. Check how the version installed on your device creates and switches between counters before relying on separate totals.

What can I count with Counter Pro?

You can use a simple tally for repeatable events such as exercise repetitions, reading sessions, stock items, visitors, scores, or daily tasks. Define one clear meaning for each count.

Can I use Counter Pro to count gym reps?

A manual counter can tally completed repetitions for exercises such as push-ups or squats. It does not replace a workout plan or a detailed training log.

Can I use Counter Pro as a tasbih counter?

A manual digital tally can help keep track of repetitions during tasbih, zikr, or dhikr. Use it in the way that best supports your own practice.

Does Counter Pro work without internet?

Offline behavior may depend on the installed version and the action you need. Check the app's current listing or test basic counting offline before relying on it.

How do I increase a count?

Open the counter for the activity and use its increment control once for each event you have decided to count. Confirm the total occasionally if accuracy matters.

How should I reset a counter safely?

Check and record any total you need before using reset. Start a fresh count only when you are ready for a new session, since reset may clear the current value.

Does Counter Pro save count history automatically?

History and persistence behavior can vary by version. Review the installed app's settings and help details, and keep a separate record of important totals until you confirm how they are saved.

Can I export my counts?

Do not assume export is available. Check the current app version for an export or backup option; if none is provided, write important totals to a separate record.

Can Counter Pro replace inventory software?

No. A tally can assist with a manual stock count, but it does not replace a full inventory system with product records, reconciliation, or stock controls.

What data or permissions does the app use?

Review the current Google Play Data safety section and the permissions requested by the installed app. These details can change between releases.

Where can I get the latest app details or help?

Visit the Counter Pro Google Play listing for current compatibility, features, pricing, and developer contact options.
